    CSV formats

    Import CSV works out which kind of file you picked from its header row. Column names aren't case-sensitive.

    Walmart products

    Required: title, price, and a upc, gtin, or mpn. Optional: oos_status (e.g. "Shipping OOS", "Low stock"), rank, reviews, walmart_url. Products you already track get their price and status updated.

    Supplier offers

    Required: upc (or gtin) and cost. Optional: source, shipping, fees, quantity, moq, condition, confidence. Each row is matched to a product you track by UPC/GTIN, so import products first.

    Supplier manifests with different column names (e.g. "Unit Cost", "Qty") work too: you'll be asked to match the columns once, and the layout is remembered for that supplier.
